Shipping & Returns

What are your shipping options?

We offer free standard shipping on all orders. Express delivery is available for a small fee. Orders are processed within 1-2 business days.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Shoes must be unworn and in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 7 days.

Our Heritage

Shandar VIP Shoes was born from a simple yet powerful vision: to craft footwear that combines timeless elegance with modern comfort. Founded by Muhammad Khurshid Alam, the brand has grown from a small workshop in Karachi to a recognized name in premium footwear across Pakistan. Every pair reflects decades of expertise, passion, and an unwavering commitment to quality.

We believe that shoes are not just accessories — they are an extension of your personality. That is why we meticulously source the finest leather, employ skilled artisans, and uphold rigorous quality checks. From the first sketch to the final stitch, our process is driven by a single goal: to make you feel extraordinary with every step.

Our Promise

We stand behind every pair we sell. Our shoes are designed to last, using only the highest-grade materials and reinforced construction. Customer satisfaction is at the core of our business — we listen, we adapt, and we deliver excellence.
